01 · Who We Serve

Built for leaders without a data team.

Aligned KPIs™ primarily serves CEOs and Executive Directors of organizations that need better visibility into their performance without building an internal data and analytics team.

Primary buyer

CEO / Executive Director

Accountable for organizational performance; needs one clear, trusted picture.

Critical stakeholder

Board & Board Chair

Often approves the investment; needs confidence that leadership has timely, trustworthy visibility.

Users & beneficiaries

Executive team & staff

Important users. Customer/member-facing visibility is a future expansion opportunity—it should not dilute our CEO and Board positioning.

02 · The Problem We Solve

Organizations don't lack data. They lack trusted, aligned Executive Visibility.

Financial data lives in accounting. Members in the CRM. Sales, marketing, operations, budgets, goals and plans in still more systems, spreadsheets and reports. It all exists—but it's fragmented.

What that causes
  • ›CEOs spend too much time gathering, reconciling and interpreting information.
  • ›Important reports arrive too late to influence decisions.
  • ›Different reports carry different definitions or numbers.
  • ›Boards struggle to understand performance quickly.
  • ›Leadership teams operate from different systems, priorities and measures.
  • ›Problems surface late; opportunities go unnoticed.
  • ›Strategic goals disconnect from the daily KPIs that drive them.
  • ›Manual reporting consumes executive and staff time; inconsistency erodes trust.

This is more than a reporting problem. It is a leadership problem.

03 · Our Core Promise

See Clearly. Lead Confidently. Stay Aligned.

Aligned KPIs turns fragmented organizational data into trusted Executive Visibility so CEOs can make better decisions, Boards can govern with confidence, and teams can stay aligned around what matters.

We create a shared, trusted view of organizational performance—from the boardroom through the leadership team.

07 · Our Two-Part Solution

Technology plus experienced executive partnership.

The technology layer

Executive Visibility Platform

Connects, aligns, calculates, monitors and presents organizational information—an enduring infrastructure for trusted visibility across financial, member/customer, sales, marketing, operational and strategic data.

Connect → Align → Calculate → Monitor → Present → Alert

One trusted data foundation powers dashboards, Board reporting, scorecards, financial reporting, presentations, analysis, forecasts, executive insights, mobile, alerts and AI-powered intelligence.

One trusted data foundation. Multiple ways to consume it.

The human expertise layer

Executive Partnership

Technology alone can't determine what to measure or what to do about it. Our Executive Partners work alongside leadership to:

  • ✓Determine what matters & define meaningful KPIs; align KPI definitions
  • ✓Connect strategy with measurement; improve executive & Board reporting
  • ✓Identify visibility gaps; integrate systems & data; interpret performance
  • ✓Facilitate planning; establish operating rhythms; improve accountability
  • ✓Continuously strengthen the organization's management system

Platform provides visibility. Partnership provides perspective. Together they create confidence and alignment.

19 · Brand Decision Filter

Before building anything, ask:

  1. Does this help the CEO see more clearly?
  2. Does it help the CEO lead more confidently?
  3. Does it create greater alignment between the CEO, Board, leadership team and organization?
  4. Does it strengthen trusted Executive Visibility?
  5. Does it help identify risks or opportunities sooner?
  6. Does it reduce the burden of gathering, reconciling or interpreting information?
  7. Does it appropriately combine technology, intelligence and human judgment?
  8. Does it reinforce Aligned KPIs as an Executive Visibility solution rather than simply another dashboard or BI product?

If yes, it's likely aligned. If it primarily creates more data, more reports, more dashboards or more complexity without improving visibility, confidence, decisions or alignment—it probably isn't.

Appendix B

The Lens (product navigation & visibility)

Visibility is a per-asset access setting (Google-Drive style). The Lens is the viewing filter along one spectrum: Only me → Team → Board → Public (Executive Committee nested under Board; Finance Team under Team). Say "visibility" for access and "Lens" for the view; avoid "persona" in product copy (personas govern brand/marketing). Canonical model: Product Spec.md.
