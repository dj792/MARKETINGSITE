01 · Our Mission

Every CEO deserves Executive Visibility.

Large enterprises employ analysts, controllers, consultants, and business intelligence teams to create it. Most chambers, associations, and growing organizations do not.

Aligned KPIs™ exists to make Executive Visibility accessible to every CEO—regardless of organization size.

02 · Brand Anchor

We don't believe CEOs need more dashboards. We believe they need better decisions.

This is our defining statement. Everything we build should reinforce it.

03 · The Category We Are Creating

Executive Visibility™

Executive Visibility is the ability for a CEO to immediately understand the health, direction, opportunities, and risks of the organization—so they can make better decisions with confidence.

  • ?Are we growing?
  • ?Are we profitable?
  • ?Is cash healthy?
  • ?Are we retaining customers?
  • ?Is the team aligned?
  • ?Where are our risks?
  • ?What deserves my attention today?

Executive Visibility is not another dashboard. It is executive confidence.

We are creating the Executive Visibility category. Large organizations create it through FP&A, controllers, BI teams, data analysts, and consultants. Aligned KPIs™ delivers the same outcome through one integrated platform.

05 · The Problem We Solve

The Visibility Gap

Large organizations employ entire teams to create executive visibility. Most organizations with fewer than 100 employees have none of them. This creates the Visibility Gap.

Leaders spend too much time collecting information instead of acting on it. Our mission is to close that gap.

06 · The Enemy

Decision Latency

Decision Latency is the time between something changing, leadership discovering it, leadership understanding it, and leadership acting.

Something changes → Leadership discovers it → Leadership understands it → Leadership acts.

Decision Latency costs organizations missed opportunities, slower growth, lower profitability, delayed accountability, and higher organizational risk. Everything Aligned KPIs™ builds should reduce Decision Latency.

07 · Why We Exist

Most CEOs make million-dollar decisions using yesterday's information.

Fragmented systems. Disconnected spreadsheets. Manual reporting. Conflicting metrics. Delayed visibility.

Large organizations solve this with analysts. We believe every CEO deserves the same clarity.

11 · Our Philosophy

Technology is the tailwind. Judgment is the edge.

Technology doesn't create better organizations. Better decisions do.

AI doesn't replace executive judgment. It amplifies it.

Software shouldn't overwhelm leaders with dashboards. It should surface what matters.

Every KPI, every recommendation, every alert should answer one question: "Will this help a CEO make a better decision today?"

19 · Product Decision Filter

Before approving any feature, ask:

  • ✓Does this improve Executive Visibility?
  • ✓Does it reduce Decision Latency?
  • ✓Does it help leaders make better decisions?

If the answer is "no," it probably doesn't belong in the product.
