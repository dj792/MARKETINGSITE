About 1 to 100 Advisors

We help chamber and association CEOs win with data.

Big corporations have entire teams turning data into better decisions. Chambers, associations, and the small businesses around them rarely do — and they pay for it in slower growth, missed risks, and gut-feel calls that could have been informed ones. We close that gap for lean organizations of 100 employees or less.

Who we work with

Chamber, association, and small-business CEOs who run lean.

Our clients lead chambers of commerce, member-based associations, and growth-focused small businesses — organizations of 100 employees or less, running lean with limited staff, limited budget, and no data team, who want an experienced operator in the room. We bring AI tools and decades of operating experience to surface the data your organization actually needs, so you can grow faster, manage risk smarter, and run with more clarity and less friction.

Founder

DJ Muller

DJ founded 1 to 100 Advisors to give small-company CEOs the same caliber of data insight that large corporations take for granted. He's spent three decades building, leading, and advising technology companies — including founding WebLink International, the first online membership platform for associations (now part of Momentive Software, serving 3,000+ chambers and associations representing over 1.5 million small businesses).

His entrepreneurial path started early: he founded his first tech company at 14 and sold it at 17 in the Apple ed-tech space. Since then, he's served 18 years as CEO of two venture-backed companies valued at over $100M, coached CEOs and senior leaders across multiple industries for more than a decade, and advised venture capital and private equity firms on strategy.
