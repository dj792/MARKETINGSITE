Referral Partner Program

Help More CEOs Lead with Confidence

Great businesses grow through trusted relationships. If you know a CEO who could benefit from clearer visibility into their business, we'd be honored if you introduced us.

At Aligned KPIs™, we believe great businesses grow through trusted relationships.

Many of our customers are introduced to us by business leaders, consultants, accountants, executive coaches, bankers, technology partners, and other professionals who know CEOs looking for better visibility into their business.

If you know a CEO or founder who could benefit from clearer financial and operational insights, we'd be honored if you introduced us.

As a thank you, we offer our Referral Partner Appreciation Program.

How It Works

1. Make an Introduction

Introduce us to a CEO, founder, executive director, or business owner who may benefit from Aligned KPIs. A simple email introduction is all it takes.

2. We Handle the Rest

We'll schedule a discovery conversation, learn about their business, and determine whether we're a good fit. Our approach is consultative and educational. There is never any obligation for the prospect to purchase.

3. Receive Your Referral Appreciation

If your referral becomes a customer, we'll send you a Referral Appreciation payment equal to:

10% of the customer's first-year contract value.

Who Makes Great Referral Partners?

Our best introductions typically come from professionals who work closely with CEOs, including:

  • Chamber of Commerce and Association professionals
  • Executive coaches
  • Fractional executives
  • CPAs and accounting firms
  • Business consultants
  • Commercial bankers
  • Business attorneys
  • Technology advisors
  • Existing Aligned KPIs customers

If you regularly work with business leaders, we'd love to connect with you.

Program Guidelines

To keep the program simple and fair:

  • The referral should be introduced before we have an active sales conversation with that organization.
  • Referral Appreciation is paid after the customer's initial invoice has been paid.
  • The referral payment is equal to 10% of the first-year contract value.
  • There is no limit to the number of organizations you may refer.
  • We reserve the right to determine referral eligibility when multiple referrals are received for the same organization.
