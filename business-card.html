Print specs

What to put on the job ticket.

  • Trim: 3.5″ × 2″ (US standard). Bleed: 0.125″ all sides → 3.75″ × 2.25″ artboard. Safe zone: keep all type 0.125″ inside trim — toggle the guides above to see both.
  • Corners: square trim, or ⅛″ rounded for the softer feel shown here.
  • Typography: Lato — Bold for the name, Regular for contact details, Light for the promise. Same family the wordmark is outlined from. Free under the SIL Open Font License.
  • Logo: place the vector file — never a screenshot, never re-typeset. Clear space of at least 25% of the mark's height on all sides; both layouts respect it.
  • Minimum size: the full mark holds together to about ¼″. Both layouts sit well above it.
  • Stock: 16–18pt uncoated or soft-touch matte. Option B also works as navy stock with white and green foil.
  • Color rule: navy and blue dominate, green appears exactly once. Don't add a second accent, and don't substitute an off-palette color.

If they want a spot color, ask for the closest PMS match to #16314F and #6FB52C and approve it on the proof.

Open questions

Decide before we order.

  • Which layout — A or B? (Or A for the team, B for DJ.)
  • Titles for the rest of the team, and whether everyone carries the same reverse.
  • Single- or double-sided, and rounded vs. square corners.
  • Quantity and stock — that drives whether foil is worth it.
Once a direction is picked, the next artifact is a print-ready PDF with bleed, crop marks and outlined type — one file per side, per person. Ask DJ.
