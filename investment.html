Common questions

Straight answers.

How fast can we go live?

Days, not months. Once we connect the systems you already use, your first live dashboards are up and running — not a quarter-long implementation.

Do I get expert help on the Starter package, or only at the top tier?

Every package includes it. Even Starter begins with an experienced executive reviewing your financials, strategy, KPIs, and reports — and handing you a profitability and growth plan with clear direction. The Executive package goes further with an ongoing, dedicated Executive Partner and regular working sessions, but you’re never buying software alone.

Can I start with Starter and add coverage later?

Yes. Most CEOs start with their most important numbers and add dashboards and scorecards as they grow. You never pay for coverage before you need it, and everything you’ve set up carries forward.

What happens after the first year?

Your platform plan includes the first year of live dashboards and connectivity monitoring. After that it renews annually to keep your data flowing and your dashboards current. We’ll walk through renewal on your call.

Is Executive Partnership just consulting?

No. It isn’t billable advisory hours or a deck-and-a-handshake. It’s an ongoing, experienced executive who already knows your numbers — because they’re looking at the same live data you are. Most CEOs don’t need another consultant. They need another experienced executive.
