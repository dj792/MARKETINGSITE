Full bio

294 words · Press, keynote programs, long-form introductions

DJ Muller is a three-time CEO and technology entrepreneur who has spent three decades building technology companies, working with boards, and helping leaders make better decisions.

He founded WebLink International, the first online membership platform for associations. Now part of Momentive Software, it became a backbone for chambers and associations across the country, growing to serve more than 3,000 organizations representing over 1.5 million businesses. That work gave him a front-row seat to how thousands of organizations actually operate, and where executive visibility consistently breaks down.

He also spent more than a decade as a CEO and alongside CEOs, investors, and operators within billion-dollar venture capital firms. Venture firms can't run every company in a portfolio themselves, so they develop disciplined frameworks for understanding performance: trusted data, consistent metrics, operating rhythms, and clear accountability. DJ adapted those practices to his own companies and then to the CEOs and organizations he advised.

Along the way he saw a gap. Large and venture-backed companies have financial teams, analysts, operating partners, and business intelligence resources. Most small and midsized organizations don't. Yet their CEOs are still expected to understand the entire business, report confidently to a board, anticipate problems, and make consequential decisions.

He founded 1 to 100 Advisors, Inc. to close that gap, and created Aligned KPIs™, an Executive Visibility Platform for CEOs delivered alongside experienced Executive Partnership. Software provides visibility. AI provides intelligence. People provide judgment.

DJ has years of experience working with boards and board chairs, has spent more than ten years coaching CEOs and senior executives across industries, and has been recognized by Microsoft as a technology innovator. His view of the work is simple: the goal isn't to give a CEO more data. It's to help them build a better company.
