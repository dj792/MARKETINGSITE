A proven methodology

Accelerating growth, retention, and profitability.

Built from decades of executive leadership experience serving chambers, associations, and membership-based organizations, our proprietary framework combines member economics, market analysis, KPI alignment, retention strategy, executive coaching, and technology deployment into one cohesive methodology.

The problem

Why most KPI initiatives fail.

  • × KPIs aren't connected to financial outcomes
  • × Teams don't understand how they contribute to success
  • × Data lives in multiple disconnected systems
  • × Reporting is reactive rather than proactive
  • × Leaders spend more time gathering information than acting on it

The result: confusion, misalignment, and missed opportunities.

The Aligned Growth Framework™

Seven phases. One operating model.

A seven-phase process designed specifically for chambers, associations, and membership-based organizations.

Phase 1

Define Success

Ideal Member Profiles & Revenue Analysis

Before measuring performance, we must understand what success looks like. We analyze:

  • Ideal Member Profiles (IMPs)
  • Revenue streams
  • Profitability by segment
  • Member value
  • Strategic objectives
Outcome

A clear understanding of where growth and profitability come from.

Phase 2

Understand the Market

Market Opportunity Assessment

We evaluate:

  • Total Addressable Market
  • Market penetration
  • Competitive positioning
  • Growth opportunities
Outcome

A focused strategy targeting the highest-value opportunities.

Phase 3

Build the Growth Engine

Growth KPI Framework

We identify the metrics that drive:

  • Membership growth
  • Revenue growth
  • Market share
  • New member acquisition
Outcome

A measurable roadmap for growth.

Phase 4

Strengthen Retention

Retention & Engagement Framework

Growth is only valuable if members stay engaged. We identify KPIs that improve:

  • Retention
  • Engagement
  • Program participation
  • Expansion revenue
  • Lifetime member value
Outcome

More loyal members and stronger recurring revenue.

Phase 5

Align KPIs to Financial Outcomes

Financial Alignment

This is where data becomes powerful. Every KPI is connected to financial outcomes including:

  • Revenue
  • Profitability
  • Cash flow
  • Sustainability
Outcome

A clear understanding of how daily activities impact financial performance.

Phase 6

Enable the Team

Leadership & Culture Development

Dashboards don't create results. People do. We help leadership build a sustainable, data-driven culture focused on:

  • Accountability
  • Transparency
  • Continuous improvement
  • Positive reinforcement
Outcome

An aligned organization focused on the metrics that matter.

Phase 7

Activate the CEO Operating System™

Technology & Visibility

We connect your existing systems to create:

  • Executive Dashboards
  • Board Reporting
  • KPI Scorecards
  • Mobile Visibility
  • AI-Powered Insights

No duplicate data entry. No additional reporting burden.

Outcome

Instant visibility into organizational performance.
