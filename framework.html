It starts with what the CEO and Board need to see, works backward to the measures that actually drive the business, and ends with those numbers in front of leadership every week — without adding a reporting burden to anyone's job.

The problem

It isn't a reporting problem.

Organizations don't lack data. What they lack is a shared, timely, trusted view of performance — so the Board, the CEO, and the leadership team end up making important decisions from different pictures of the same organization.

  • × Financial, membership, and operational data live in disconnected systems
  • × Reports arrive after the month has closed, too late to change the outcome
  • × Different reports carry different definitions and different numbers
  • × The Board struggles to see how the organization is really performing
  • × Problems surface only after they have become bigger problems
  • × Leaders spend more time assembling information than acting on it

It's a leadership problem. And it's the one this method is built to solve.

  • Strategic objectives
