The Lens

How the platform organizes what a CEO sees and controls who can see it. Canonical reference — use these names and definitions consistently.

Internal product spec · see “Product Spec.md” · last updated Aug 2026

Two concepts, kept separate

Property of the asset

Visibility

The access control on each asset (dashboard, scorecard, analysis, document, folder) — who can open it. Works like a Google Drive share setting and travels with the asset wherever it's linked.

A way of looking

The Lens

A view the CEO looks through. It filters what's shown by each asset's visibility. The Lens is not a permission — switching it never changes who can access anything.

The privacy spectrum — most closed to most open

Only me
The CEO alone — drafts, personal dashboards, pre-decision work.
Team
Internal staff and key vendors.
Board
Board of directors and investors.
Public
Anyone on the web — world-readable, indexable.

Governing principles

  1. Visibility is a property of the asset; the Lens is how you look. Never merge the two — moving an asset between views must not silently re-permission it.
  2. Nesting rule: a subgroup sees a superset of its parent (Executive Committee ⊂ Board; Finance Team ⊂ Team).
  3. Default visibility is “Only me.” New assets start private; the CEO deliberately widens them.
  4. Sharing = widening one step outward (Only me → Team → Board → Public). Promoting to Public is an explicit, confirmed action.
  5. The spectrum doubles as the maturation pipeline — draft privately, then promote outward as content is ready.
Say visibility for the per-asset access property and Lens for the viewing filter. Executive Committee and Finance Team are subgroups, not top-level Lens values. “Persona” was an early working term these concepts replaced — don’t use it in product copy.
