04 · How It Works

From QuickBooks to the CEO's dashboard.

Most financial numbers start in the customer's QuickBooks Online. Nobody re-types them: QuickBooks emails the reports to us, and the rest happens on its own.

1Reports scheduledP&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, Budget vs. Actuals — set to email on a schedule.

5Sheet updatedThe report replaces last month's copy in that customer's own sheet.
6Date stampedThe report's "as of" date is recorded.

7Job rebuildsReads the sheet and builds a ready summary.
8CEO sees itDashboards show the new month, with its source and date.
Every morning all dashboards rebuild automatically. Need it now? Admin → Jobs → Rebuild. Something missing? Check Admin → Sync Log first.
  1. Set up once per customer. In QuickBooks, the customer (or we) schedule their reports to email to our extraction mailbox. In Aligned KPIs, Admin → Extractions records which reports to expect and which sheet each goes to.
  2. Reports arrive by themselves. The email extractor only accepts mail from approved senders, so a stray email can't overwrite a customer's numbers.
  3. The Google Sheet is the hand-off point. Each customer has their own sheets in their own confidential folder. Admin → Data Sources shows which sheet each dashboard reads — and refuses a sheet that belongs to another customer.
  4. Jobs turn sheets into dashboards. A job reads a sheet and saves a finished summary, so dashboards load fast and never touch the raw sheet while a CEO is looking.

Other systems follow the same shape with a different first step: GrowthZone reports are downloaded by an automated browser that signs in for us, and some systems will connect directly by API. Everything still lands in a Google Sheet, so the jobs and dashboards don't change.

05 · Good Habits

A few rules that keep customers' data safe.

Check whose screen you're on

  • ✓Staff switch between customers often. Before you save anything, read the organization name in the header.
  • ✓Each customer's sheets belong to that customer only — never reuse a link from another account.

Passwords are write-only

  • ✓System passwords (e.g. GrowthZone) are entered in Admin → Extractions and stored securely. Nobody — including us — can view them afterward.
  • ✓Never put a password in a sheet, an email or a note.

Look before you rebuild

  • ✓If a dashboard looks stale, check the source date and the Sync Log first — a rebuild can't fix a report that never arrived.

Ask early

  • ✓Unsure about a customer's numbers or setup? Ask DJ before changing anything. A quick question beats a confused CEO.
