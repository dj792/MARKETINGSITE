Privacy Policy

Last updated
Draft for review. This policy is a working draft and has not yet been reviewed by legal counsel. Please review and have it approved before relying on it.

This Privacy Policy explains how 1 to 100 Advisors, Inc. ("1 to 100," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the Aligned KPIs™ platform and related services (the "Services"). By using the Services, you agree to the practices described here.

Our core promise

Your numbers are yours. We never sell or rent your data, and we never share it with, or use it to train, third-party AI models. We use your data only to provide and improve the Services for you.

Information we collect

  • Account information — name, email, phone, organization, and role.
  • Connected business data — information you authorize us to access from systems you connect, such as accounting, CRM/membership, marketing, and operational platforms, used to build your dashboards and reports.
  • Usage information — how you interact with the Services, including log data, device and browser information, and analytics.
  • Communications — information you provide when you contact us or request a demo.

How we use information

  • To provide, operate, secure, and improve the Services.
  • To build and maintain your executive dashboards, KPIs, and reports.
  • To communicate with you about your account, support, and updates.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

How we share information

We share information only with service providers who help us operate the Services (for example, hosting and infrastructure providers), under contracts that require them to protect it; when required by law; or in connection with a business transfer. We do not sell your data.

Third-party integrations

When you connect a third-party system, we access only the data needed to deliver the Services and in line with the permissions you grant. Your use of those third-party systems remains governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Data security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and to limit access to those who need it to deliver the Services.

Data retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the Services and for legitimate business or legal purposes. You may request deletion of your data as described below.

Cookies and analytics

We may use cookies and similar technologies to operate the site, remember preferences, and understand usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To make a request, contact us at dj@1to100advisors.com.

Children's privacy

The Services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here and revise the "last updated" date above.
