What changed

  • Flywheel. Two equal 147° arcs with openings at the lower left and upper right. The break makes it read as turning rather than closed — and gives the plane a doorway on the way out.
  • Pillars. Four bars on one baseline, ascending in height and tone (Blue 40% → 70% → Brand Blue → Navy). Heights now accelerate — 33 / 45 / 61 / 84 — so the pillars climb with the trend instead of stepping evenly.
  • Trend line. A true accelerating climb: it enters at 22° and steepens smoothly to 52° at the exit, while holding a constant ~6.4px above every pillar. Nothing is crossed or clipped.
  • The plane. No lower wing. The trend line's underside runs unbroken into the nose as the keel, with a single wing swept back above it — the profile of a paper plane rather than the barbs of an arrow. The keel carries the same slight curve as the line's own edge, so the join is seamless.
  • Palette. Only Brand Strategy Appendix D tokens and tints of them. No off-palette color.
